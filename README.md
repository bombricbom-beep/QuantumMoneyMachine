# LeadFlow AI

LeadFlow AI is a real SaaS MVP for local service businesses that want to respond to leads faster, qualify better, and stop losing prospects.

## Product idea

A business receives leads from WhatsApp, web forms, ads, and call tracking. LeadFlow AI automatically:
- answers the lead instantly
- qualifies the lead with a structured questionnaire
- routes the lead to the right salesperson
- summarizes the conversation
- tracks lead conversion and follow-up performance

## Why this is a real business

This is not a fake simulation. The product solves a concrete pain point:
- local businesses lose leads when they respond too late
- most leads never get followed up
- sales teams are overloaded and inconsistent

A business pays monthly for speed, consistency, and pipeline capture.

## MVP goals

- landing page for conversion
- lead capture form
- pricing section
- SaaS backend for processing requests
- basic admin metrics
- real integration path for WhatsApp, email, and CRM later

## Stack
